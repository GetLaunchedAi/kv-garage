Partnership Tiers

Choose Your Partnership Level

We offer three distinct partnership tiers, each designed to align with different goals, investment levels, and strategic objectives. Whether you're looking to grow your business, invest capital, or distribute at scale, we have a partnership structure for you.

Growth Partner

Growth Partner

Ideal for resellers and businesses looking to scale their operations through strategic wholesale partnerships. Access exclusive inventory, priority support, and growth-focused resources.

  • Exclusive access to premium inventory packs
  • Priority customer support and account management
  • Flexible payment terms and deposit options
  • Early access to new products and deals
  • Volume discounts and tiered pricing
  • Marketing resources and co-branding opportunities
  • Quarterly business reviews and growth planning
Requirements:

Minimum $10K monthly order volume, established reseller business, references

Capital Partner

Capital Partner

For investors and financial partners looking to participate in the growth of the tech supply network. Strategic capital partnerships that fuel expansion, inventory acquisition, and market penetration.

  • Equity participation or revenue-sharing arrangements
  • Priority access to high-value inventory opportunities
  • Transparent financial reporting and performance metrics
  • Board-level advisory opportunities (at higher tiers)
  • Co-investment opportunities in strategic acquisitions
  • Exclusive access to proprietary market data and insights
  • Network access to other partners and opportunities
Requirements:

Minimum $50K investment, accredited investor status preferred, strategic alignment

Distribution Partner

Distribution Partner

For established distributors, logistics companies, and regional partners who can help us scale operations, reach new markets, and expand our distribution network across geographic regions.

  • Exclusive regional distribution rights
  • White-label and co-branding options
  • Dedicated inventory allocation and fulfillment support
  • Marketing co-op funds and promotional support
  • Technology platform integration and API access
  • Joint marketing and lead generation programs
  • Strategic expansion planning and market development
Requirements:

Established distribution infrastructure, minimum $100K monthly commitment, logistics capability
