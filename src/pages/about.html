About Kavion Steele

Built from the Ground Up

I wasn't born into opportunity — I built it. At four years old, I entered foster care. By seven, I was adopted into a new home and a new life. That experience taught me one thing early: no one's coming to save you — you have to build your own foundation. Growing up, I learned how to create stability where there was none. Every challenge became training. Every setback built muscle. I learned how to lead myself before I ever led others.

From my first jobs, I didn't just clock in — I took over. I became the person people turned to when things had to get done. Whether it was running small teams, directing crews, or managing workflow, I always found a way to make systems better. In every job I had, I rose fast — not because I was chasing promotion, but because I cared about performance. I turned every role into a business within a business. By 20, I was leading factory teams, training older coworkers, and earning six-figure offers as a future manager.

That's when it clicked: if I could build million-dollar productivity for someone else, I could build it for myself. I had mastered sales — door-to-door, phone, and high-pressure environments — and consistently ranked as the top performer everywhere I worked. Once I understood that selling was just leadership in motion, I knew it was time to go all in on my own vision.

I launched KV Garage to prove what discipline and vision can create from nothing. It started as a wholesale tech-accessory business but quickly evolved into something more — a system for independence. Today, KV Garage isn't just about selling products; it's about teaching others how to build freedom through reselling, trading, and systems thinking. I'm not here to impress people — I'm here to impact them.

I teach because I remember being the kid who had to figure it out alone. I know what it's like to start from zero, to build trust, to fight for consistency. That's why I mentor others — not from theory, but from lived experience. Whether it's sales, trading, or leadership, I help people create income systems that give them real choices. My goal isn't just to build a business. It's to build people who build legacies.

From foster care to founder — I built my life the same way I build my businesses: from the bottom up, with vision, systems, and heart.

Kavion Steele Founder & CEO
